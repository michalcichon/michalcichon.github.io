02 / Principles

How I approach engineering.

Technology is a means to an end, and good architecture is ultimately about making complex systems reliable, understandable, and maintainable.

Reliability over Autonomy

I prefer systems that are predictable and controllable over systems that are autonomous simply for the sake of being autonomous.

Systems over Features

I think in terms of architecture, boundaries, failure modes, and long-term maintainability — not just individual features.

Humans in the Loop

AI should augment human expertise rather than obscure responsibility or remove meaningful control from the system.

Pragmatism

Performance, cost, privacy, operational complexity, and user trust are not secondary concerns. They are fundamental engineering constraints.
