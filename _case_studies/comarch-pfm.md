## Context
The frontend was an Angular single-page app optimized for banking data visualization. The backend, in Spring and Hibernate, categorized bank transactions. I was involved from 2011 to 2013.

## My role
I worked on every layer of the system. I implemented new descriptors for the PFM framework and integrated with external bank services. The team worked in Scrum with regular demos and retros.
