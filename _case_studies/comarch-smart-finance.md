## Context
A banking application deployed at Splitska Banka and at one of the biggest banks in Poland. I was involved from 2012 to 2016.

## What I worked on
- Heavy optimization for offline mode.
- UI built both in XIBs and in code.
- Many custom views, tuned for usability and performance.

## Codebase
Objective-C throughout.
