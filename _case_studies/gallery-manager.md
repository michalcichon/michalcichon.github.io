## Context
Gallery Manager is an iOS app for browsing an art gallery's inventory. I worked on it between June and December 2016.

## What I worked on
- Integrating the app with the new backend.
- Fixing performance issues.

## What it does
- Favorites and a full list of collections, with search.
- Artwork details: artist, title, medium, size, gallery, availability and price.
- Sending an artwork to someone from the details screen.
