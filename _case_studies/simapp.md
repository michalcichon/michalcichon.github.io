## Context
SIMAPP is an iPad tool for scientific sales reps who sell medical devices. It was released to hundreds of users across multiple global regions. I worked on it in 2016.

## What I worked on
- Performance issues, bug fixes and crash investigations.
- Advanced multi-level filtering of content.
- Faster content loading.
- Fixes for animation glitches.
- An integrated email composer.
- An advanced scientific calculator.
